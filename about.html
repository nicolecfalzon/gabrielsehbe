About Sehbe Advisors

Independent Advice. Lasting Relationships.

Since 2010

A Boutique Firm

Founded in 2010, Sehbe Advisors is a boutique strategic advisory and capital markets consulting firm with offices in New York and São Paulo.

The firm provides customized advisory and transaction support to companies, entrepreneurs, investors and strategic partners across a range of industries, asset classes and private-market opportunities.
