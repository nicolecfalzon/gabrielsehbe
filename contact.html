Gabriel Sehbe is the Chief Executive Officer of Sehbe Advisors LLC, a strategic advisory and capital markets consulting firm serving clients and partners across the United States and Brazil.

With extensive experience in strategic advisory, capital markets, business development, and cross-border transactions, Gabriel works closely with entrepreneurs, companies, investors, and strategic partners to identify opportunities, develop transaction strategies, and facilitate relationships that support long-term growth.

His work includes advising on capital raising, investor positioning, strategic partnerships, business development, and the structuring and execution of complex transactions across multiple sectors and asset classes, including real estate, hospitality, debt instruments, fixed-income securities, and other private-market opportunities.

Based in New York, Gabriel has developed a broad network of relationships across the United States and Brazil, with a particular focus on connecting Brazilian businesses and investment opportunities with international investors and strategic partners.

Gabriel founded Sehbe Advisors in 2010 with the objective of providing clients with an independent, discreet, and highly customized approach to strategic advisory. He remains actively involved in the firm's client relationships, transaction development, and strategic initiatives in both markets.
